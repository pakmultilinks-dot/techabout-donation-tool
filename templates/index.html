CSV format

The first row must be a header with these columns: name, amount, date.

name,amount,date
Ahmed Khan,5000,2026-09-15
Sara Malik,2500,2026-09-16

Dates accept YYYY-MM-DD (also DD/MM/YYYY). Amounts must be positive numbers. Bad rows are listed and skipped, good rows still make the report.
